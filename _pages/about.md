I am a Pappalardo Fellow at MIT, in the Center for Theoretical Physics.

I study the physics of quantum field theories. The goal is to understand behaviors like the [confinement of quarks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Color_confinement) in quantum chromodynamics using non-perturbative methods. I primarily work on applying Hamiltonian lattice gauge theory to low-dimensional gauge theories, and applying the conformal bootstrap to superconformal field theories. See my [publications](/publications) for more details. I am also involved in [outreach efforts](/outreach), including the National Science Bowl. 

Previously, my notes "Undergraduate Physics in a Hurry" were hosted here. These notes have become a book with Princeton Press, co-authored with [Andy Leifer](http://leiferlab.princeton.edu) and now titled [Undergraduate Physics: Four Years in One](https://press.princeton.edu/books/paperback/9780691260549/undergraduate-physics).

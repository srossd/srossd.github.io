- [**Undergraduate Physics in a Hurry**](/assets/pdf/Undergraduate_Physics_in_a_Hurry.pdf): I wrote these notes in 2019 to prepare for the preliminary exam in physics that all physics Ph.D. students at Princeton take. It's a rapid-fire review of mechanics, electromagnetism, quantum mechanics, and statistical mechanics, with around 120 worked example problems. I am in the process of turning this into a book with Princeton Press, co-authored with [Andy Leifer](http://leiferlab.princeton.edu). When that is complete, the draft version will no longer be available here.
- [**Mathematica Hogwarts: Becoming a Wizard**](/assets/notebooks/WintersessionMathematica.nb): The Mathematica notebook for a workshop I taught during Princeton's Wintersession. Here is a [version](/assets/notebooks/WintersessionMathematicaSolved.nb) with the exercises solved.
- [**Introduction to quantum mechanics**](/assets/pdf/Quantum_Mechanics.pdf): Some notes on quantum mechanics I wrote as an undergraduate for some friends.
